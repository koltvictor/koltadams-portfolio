Kolt's Table

Seasonal cooking platform. Solo, six months, brand through infrastructure.

Problem
Seasonal food content goes stale in weeks. One person can't keep a site current by hand, and a site that isn't current is worthless.
The call
Make the site rotate itself. A seasonal engine turns the homepage, produce guides, navigation, and editorial over by month. Behind it, a 20-job intelligence network — jobs trigger each other through tracked causal links, not independent crons — drafts, scores, and proposes. Every AI write waits in a human approval queue. Nothing touches the database without me.
What it cost
A 60-table normalized schema and a governance layer before the first recipe published. Slower to start; nothing to unwind later.
Proof
Production bundle 180 KB where NYT Cooking ships 380. Search responds under 50 ms. Lighthouse 96. Natural-language recipe search and ingredient matching run on Claude in production today.
React 19 · TypeScript · Node · PostgreSQL · Redis · Docker · Claude · Gemini koltstable.com ↗

Toren

Native iOS supplement and longevity tracker. Private beta.

Problem
Supplement-interaction data online is confidently wrong. A tracker that repeats it is worse than no tracker.
The call
AI-drafted, clinician-gated, schema-enforced. A database-level constraint makes it structurally impossible to publish a high-severity interaction warning backed only by preliminary evidence — the safety invariant lives in the schema, not in reviewer discretion.
What it cost
Every record read by a licensed physician; 7 of 107 retracted, and the systematic errors that produced them fixed at the source. A pharmacist completeness review is a hard gate before public launch, not a nice-to-have.
Proof
100 published interaction records across 41 substances, each human-reviewed. Nightly biometric ingestion from Oura and Apple HealthKit, with per-source retention honoring each provider's terms. In private TestFlight with an active household of testers.
SwiftUI · HealthKit · Node · PostgreSQL · Cloudflare Access · Claude torenapp.com ↗

Inkling

Mental-health screening orientation. Helps someone decide whether what they're feeling is worth a professional conversation.

Problem
A model that guesses wrong here does harm in both directions — missing a crisis, or medicalizing a bad week.
The call
Two model stages, neither of which diagnoses. The first reads a person's own words and routes them to a validated screener — or straight to crisis support when the language calls for it. The second reflects results in plain language that points toward care. Accountless, on-device, no personal data collected.
What it cost
A 46-profile evaluation suite with a crisis-detection floor that had to hold through every prompt revision, plus 42 out-of-scope guards against misrouting. Prompts that didn't clear the floor didn't ship.
Proof
46 of 46 profiles passing. Crisis support one tap from every screen. Shipped to TestFlight.
React Native · Expo · TypeScript · Cloudflare Workers · Claude inklingapp.org ↗

Lopud Management

Client work. A talent management firm representing world-class talent, where the brand guidelines carry veto power.

Problem
The brief was restraint: expensive, weightless, nothing corporate. Every use of the brand is governed by an identity-guideline document that vetoes shadows, recolors, and overlap.
The call
No framework. No analytics, no cookie banner, no about page. Static files, a Makefile, and a screenshot-diff verifier so a stylesheet change can't regress a page nobody looked at. The signature mark machine-traced from the original scan and approved under those guidelines.
What it cost
Deleting a Vite/Tailwind scaffold that was never doing anything, and rebuilding deploy around what the host actually supports.
Proof
Live, indexed, and pixel-verified against its own previous release on every deploy.
HTML · CSS · Make · Playwright lopudmanagement.com ↗
